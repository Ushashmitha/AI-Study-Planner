# AI Study Planner

## 1. Project Title

AI Study Planner

## 2. Project Objective

To develop a web-based AI-powered study planning system that creates personalized study schedules for students based on their subjects, topics, exam dates, topic difficulty, preparation level, available study time, and study preferences.

The system should adapt the study plan when the student's progress or availability changes.

## 3. Target Users

College students who need help organizing their academic preparation.

## 4. Problem

Students often struggle to decide:

* What topic to study
* How much time to spend on each topic
* Which topics should receive higher priority
* How to prepare before multiple exams
* How to adjust their schedule when they miss study sessions

## 5. Proposed Solution

The application will collect information about the student's academic workload and available study time.

A planning algorithm will calculate topic priorities and generate a feasible study schedule.

AI will be used to provide personalized study recommendations, explain why certain topics are prioritized, and assist with adapting the student's study plan.

## Important Design Principle

The database stores the student's academic information and study history.

The planning algorithm uses this information to generate valid study sessions.

AI is not responsible for directly modifying database records without validation.

# 12. Study Planning Algorithm

The study planner will use a priority-based scheduling algorithm.

## Priority Factors

Each topic will receive a priority score based on:

* Exam urgency: 35%
* Topic difficulty: 20%
* Topic importance: 15%
* Remaining work: 15%
* Student weakness/confidence: 15%

## Normalization

Difficulty and importance will use a 1–5 scale.

Completion will use a 0–100% scale.

Confidence will use a 1–5 scale.

Remaining work will be calculated as:

Remaining Work = 1 - Completion

Weakness will be calculated as:

Weakness = 1 - Normalized Confidence

## Exam Urgency

Initial urgency levels:

* 0–2 days: 1.00
* 3–5 days: 0.90
* 6–10 days: 0.70
* 11–20 days: 0.50
* 21–30 days: 0.30
* More than 30 days: 0.15

These values can be adjusted during testing.

## Planning Process

1. Retrieve student information.
2. Calculate exam urgency for each subject.
3. Calculate priority for each topic.
4. Sort topics according to priority.
5. Retrieve the student's available study slots.
6. Allocate study time to high-priority topics.
7. Divide large study requirements into multiple sessions.
8. Add breaks according to user preferences.
9. Validate the generated schedule.
10. Prevent time conflicts and overlapping sessions.
11. Prevent exceeding the student's available study time.
12. Prevent scheduling preparation after the relevant examination.
13. Save the generated sessions.
14. Update priorities when the student provides new progress information.

## Adaptive Behaviour

The system will adapt when:

* A study session is completed.
* A study session is skipped.
* Topic completion changes.
* Student confidence changes.
* Examination dates approach.
* Student availability changes.

## AI Responsibility

The deterministic planning algorithm will remain responsible for creating valid schedules.

The AI model will be used for:

* Personalized recommendations.
* Explaining study priorities.
* Daily study advice.
* Identifying areas requiring attention.
* Helping the student understand their study progress.

AI-generated recommendations will be validated before affecting the actual study schedule.
